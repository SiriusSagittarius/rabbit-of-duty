Pack description
----------------

This is a pack of recordings of me screaming myself hoarse into a microphone. I wanted to capture some deep, full-bodied pain sounds to use in game projects. From the more muted grunts to dramatic, clichéd screams of &#x27;Noooo!&#x27;, this pack should give you some nice material for an action game.
